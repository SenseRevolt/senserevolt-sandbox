About This Resource

A Complete Somatic Anger Toolkit
for Adults, Therapists & Educators

The Somatic Anger Kit is a body-based, printable PDF workbook designed to help individuals move through anger safely and effectively. Unlike talk-based approaches, somatic tools work directly with the nervous system — addressing rage, frustration, and irritability at their physiological root.

Created by two sisters from Ukraine, this kit draws from lived experience navigating extreme stress alongside professional training in somatic therapy, psychology, and education. Every page is intentional, evidence-informed, and designed to be immediately usable.

Whether you're a therapist looking for ready-made session tools, a teacher or school counselor supporting students with emotional regulation, or an individual seeking healthier ways to process anger — this kit meets you where you are.

Available as an instant digital download on Etsy, Amazon KDP, and Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT). Multiple license tiers available for personal, classroom, school-wide, and commercial use.

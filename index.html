FAQs

We’ve gathered our most frequently asked questions below. For a full list of answers organized by category, feel free to visit our dedicated FAQ page.

01 What size are the digital files, and what do I need to print them?

No special equipment needed — any home printer works perfectly. Upon purchase you'll receive files in two formats:

US Letter (8.5 × 11") — standard for North America
A4 (210 × 297 mm) — standard for Europe and the rest of the world

As for paper: standard office paper works just fine. For a richer somatic and artistic experience, we recommend 100–120 gsm paper — it holds markers better and feels more intentional under your hands. But ultimately, the practice is yours to shape.

02 What license do I receive with my purchase?

All our products come with a clearly defined license. Choose the one that fits your use:

Personal Use — Individual buyer. Personal use only, not for use with clients or students.
Individual Therapist — 1 therapist / coach / educator. Use with clients in a private practice setting.
Small Group (up to 5) — Small practice or organization. Up to 5 practitioners within one organization.
School / Org — School or organization. Unlimited practitioners within one organization.

Not sure which license fits your situation? Reach out — we'll help you choose.

03 Are somatic tools suitable for beginners with no therapy background?

Absolutely. Our somatic kits are designed for independent use — no prior therapy experience needed. Each kit includes clear instructions and guided practices so you can start immediately, wherever you are in your journey.

04 What age are the SEL tools designed for?

Our SEL tools are created for children ages 4–12, with content carefully structured by developmental stage. Each product clearly indicates the recommended age range so you can choose what fits your child best.

05 Can I use these tools in a classroom or clinical setting?

Yes. Our tools are actively used by therapists, school counselors, and educators. For group or institutional use, check our TPT store for educational licensing options — including Small Group and School/Org licenses that cover your entire team or classroom.

06 What's the difference between the digital kits and the physical books?

They're not the same product — and that's intentional.

Our digital kits (available on Etsy and TPT) are focused, practice-ready tools — concentrated somatic or SEL content designed to print, use, and repeat as many times as you need.

Our physical books are a different experience entirely — 200+ pages, deeper content, a complete journey rather than a single session tool. The children's SEL books are available on Amazon. Our somatic books have their own distinct format and availability.

Think of the digital kits as your daily practice tool, and the books as your full immersion guide.

07 Do you ship internationally?

Physical books ship worldwide through Amazon — availability depends on your local Amazon marketplace. Digital downloads are available instantly everywhere, no waiting and no shipping costs.
